    How to use it

    1. Get the plan as JSON:
      EXPLAIN FORMAT=JSON <query>; on MySQL
      EXPLAIN (FORMAT JSON) <query>; on PostgreSQL
    2. Copy the result and paste it in the box above.
    3. Export the diagram as SVG (vector) or PNG.

    On PostgreSQL, adding ANALYZE brings the real execution numbers, and the diagram starts comparing the estimate against what happened.

    Nothing is sent to any server: everything runs in your browser.
